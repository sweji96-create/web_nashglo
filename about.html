Three Decades of Industrial Excellence

Founded in 1994, Nashco Global has established itself as a premier provider of comprehensive industrial solutions across the GCC region.

Our journey began with a vision to deliver world-class chemicals, automotive solutions, and trading services to the rapidly growing Gulf markets. Today, we stand as a trusted partner for businesses seeking innovative solutions in chemicals, automotive parts, complete manufacturing automation, and international trading.

With a deep understanding of the GCC market dynamics and stringent quality standards, Nashco Global has successfully completed over 1,000 projects, serving more than 500 satisfied clients across all six GCC countries.

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional industrial solutions that empower businesses across the GCC region through innovation, quality, and complete customer satisfaction. We are committed to providing premium chemicals, cutting-edge automotive solutions, state-of-the-art manufacturing automation, and strategic trading services that drive sustainable growth for our clients.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and innovative industrial solutions provider in the GCC, recognized for our commitment to excellence, complete automation expertise, and unwavering dedication to client success. We aspire to set new benchmarks in chemicals, automotive, manufacturing, and trading sectors through continuous innovation and strategic partnerships.
